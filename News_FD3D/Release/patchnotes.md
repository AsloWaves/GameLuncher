# Fathoms Deep — Patch Notes

## v1.0.3 — 2026-07-05
- New ships at the dock: the USS Bainbridge destroyer and the S-Class submarine can now be selected from the main menu (submarine is early — expect rough edges).
- Your ship now stays in the world when you log out: it keeps burning fuel and founders if the bunker runs dry — top up before you sign off. Reconnect to reclaim it right where you left it.
- Tactical map interaction restored — pan, zoom, and click work again from the commander's chair.
- New digital DOCK button on the MFD Dock page, usable in both the Area of Protection and Area of Influence.
- Ship handling: progressive heel into turns (no more instant max lean) and realistic turn rates for the Bainbridge.
- Bainbridge wake and buoyancy tuning; fixed spawning/reconnecting underwater.

## v1.0.0 — 2026-06-20
- Initial Fathoms Deep release.
- New naval-themed launcher: account login, live server status + population, and graphics/launch options.
- Minimize-to-tray, auto-start, and "What's New" highlights after each update.

## v0.9.0 (Beta) — 2026-05-15
- Closed beta: core patching pipeline and login flow online.
- Added the live server-status indicator.
- Performance pass on the news feed and image cache.
